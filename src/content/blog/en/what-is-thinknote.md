ThinkNote is here to help your brain **keep up with AI**.

Models are fast. Ask once, and an answer arrives. The answer can come from the model. Judgment has to stay with you. If you take the result and close the tab, it barely happened for your brain: no processing, no encoding; no encoding, and sleep has nothing to consolidate. The smoother the chat, the thinner the thinking.

## 🧠 Direct experience is expensive. Indirect experience is the bulk.

You can learn by doing, by teaching, by using something again and again. Those routes work. They are also expensive: they need a place, a counterpart, a stretch of time. They are not available anywhere, anytime. A lifetime only holds so much first-hand experience. What we actually live on is second-hand — books, classes, other people’s words, and now AI.

Having been there does not mean you understand. First-hand can still be a bias, a fluke, a story that felt true. Never having been there does not mean you cannot understand. Correct-enough knowledge takes reflection, and a method you can run again. Indirect experience, if you follow it up and restate it in your own words, can still become your judgment.

AI made indirect experience abundant and cheap. The shortage is not another reply. It is that method, available while you sit there. Writing is using it.

What goes into a note has to be something you chewed. Preferably your own sentence. Dumping a whole answer looks like noting. It is still handing the thinking over.

Leave a trace during the day, and memory can do its night shift. The sidepanel is not an archive. It is raw material for tonight’s brain.

## ✍️ Chat ↔ Think, then Note, Share

```
Chat ↔ Think
    → Note
    → Share
```

![Chat ↔ Think, then Note, Share](/images/uploads/what-is-thinknote-loop.png)

**Chat and Think go back and forth.** After an answer, pause: which line do you trust, which step is still foggy, what can you say in your own words. Then ask the next question. That is the method — running experience through once more, whether you lived it or the model just handed it to you.

**Note is encoding.** Write when the spark shows up. That is when memory starts working.

**Share is sending thought outward.** For you to revisit later. For someone else to open. A draft that has to meet a person is harder to fake. Right before it meets them, you suddenly get serious. That is a good thing. To explain it, you have to think one layer further: where you still do not understand, which sentence would not survive a question. Feynman’s trick works for the same reason — teaching someone else is how you find out whether you can. Sharing makes you think more, and deeper, because there is a real person on the other side.

I grow by thinking, writing things down, and looking back. I still need doing, teaching, and use — they are just not always at hand. I hope this loop helps anyone who wants to keep the thinking on their side.

The line is: **Help your brain keep up with AI. Chat, think, note, share.**

In Chinese: *让大脑跟得上 AI。边聊边想，即记即享。*

## 🛠️ Four moves

- **Ask Precisely**: prompt with formulas and structure in Precise Chat, instead of vague follow-ups in a normal box.
- **Think Deep**: lift the useful fragment (formulas included) and ask again. Catch it in your own words. Do not swallow the whole reply.
- **Note Now**: write in the sidepanel while you chat. Local and offline; cloud sync after login, within quota.
- **Share Now**: export, or paste into desktop Word / WPS in one click. Formulas stay native and editable. That can be a note you already arranged, or the chat itself — if your work already lives in Word, you need it back in the doc, fast.

This is not another chatbot. The models are still ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Doubao, DeepSeek, Yuanbao, Qianwen, Yiyan. ThinkNote is the **notebook and paste layer** that sits on those pages.

## 📦 What to install

1. **Browser extension** (Chrome / Edge): floating button and sidepanel on AI sites, plus copy and Precise Chat. Install: [thinknote.pro/install](https://thinknote.pro/install)
2. **Word / WPS add-in**: for most people whose work already lives in Word. Paste the AI chat in one click, into the workflow they already have. Not something you install only when handing work in. A lot of the job is transactional — it has to be fast, and it does not need a deep think every time. Desktop Word (Win / Mac) or WPS Writer on Windows. Notes: [thinknote.pro/word](https://thinknote.pro/word)

Word on the web is out of scope. The extension covers chat and notes. If it has to land in Word, install the add-in too.
