The happiest moment with AI is often the one you leave empty-handed.

You ask once. The answer arrives, neat as a printout still warm. You nod. You close the window. The glow is still there. The next day you try to tell someone, and all you have left is: “Yeah, it kind of made sense.”

The model is fast. Fast enough that the human part gets skipped — no pause, no chewing it over, no saying it in your own words. That's what ThinkNote is trying to help with: **letting your brain keep up with AI.**

Answers can come from the model. What you make of them has to stay with you.

## 🧠 Most of what you know, you only heard.

Doing, teaching, using something over and over — those work. They're also expensive. You need a place, someone across from you, a stretch of free time. In one life, the things you actually put your hands on are not that many.

What we really live on is what we heard — books, classes, a line someone said in passing, and now AI. It made that kind of knowledge cheap. Cheap enough that you swallow a whole reply without noticing.

Being there doesn't mean you understand. Things you lived through can still be a bias, a fluke, or a story that happened to be right once. Never being there doesn't mean you can't understand. A clearer take comes from stopping, then saying it in your own words.

If you follow up on something you heard, and say it again in your own sentence, it can still become yours. Writing it down is doing that.

What belongs in a note is a line you've already chewed on. Pasting the whole reply looks like work. You're still handing the thinking over. Leave a trace in the sidepanel during the day, and tonight your head has something to sort. The sidepanel isn't a filing cabinet. It's a pocket: when a good line shows up, tuck it in.


## ✍️ Chat ↔ Think, then Note, Share

```
Chat ↔ Think
    → Note
    → Share
    ↺ re-edit
```

![Chat ↔ Think, then Note, Share](/images/uploads/what-is-thinknote-loop.webp)

**Chat and Think go back and forth.** After an answer, pause: which line do you trust, which step is still fuzzy, what can you say in your own words. Then ask the next question. Like a conversation. Also like saying a line you just heard back to yourself. After that, it's yours.

**Note is catching the spark.** Write when it shows up. Once it's on the page, it stays — otherwise tomorrow you only remember that it felt bright.

**Share is sending the thought out.** For you later. For someone else to open. A draft that has to meet a person is harder to fake. Right before it does, you suddenly get serious. That's a good thing. To explain it, you have to think one layer further: where you still don't get it, which sentence wouldn't survive a question. The Feynman trick people talk about runs on the same pressure — teaching someone else is how you find out whether you can. Sharing makes you think more, and deeper, because there's a real person on the other side.

I've gotten a little further by thinking, writing things down, and looking back. Doing, teaching, using matter too — they're just not always nearby. I hope this loop helps anyone who also wants to keep the thinking on their side.

That's the whole pitch, kept simple: **Help your brain keep up with AI. Chat, think, note, share.**

In Chinese: *让大脑跟得上 AI。边聊边想，即记即享。*

## 🛠️ Four things it actually does

- **Ask Precisely**: ask with the formula and the structure in Precise Chat, instead of a vague “say that part again” in the normal box.
- **Think Deep**: pull out the bit that actually helps (formulas included) and ask again. Catch it in your own words. Don't swallow the whole reply.
- **Note Now**: write in the sidepanel while you chat. Notes can stay on this machine and work offline; after you log in, they sync to the cloud within your account quota.
- **Share Now**: export, or paste into desktop Word / WPS in one click. Formulas become native, so you can still edit them. That can be a note you already arranged, or the chat itself — if your work already lives in a document, you need it back there, fast.

This isn't another chatbot. The models are still ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Doubao, DeepSeek, Yuanbao, Qianwen, Yiyan. ThinkNote is the **notebook and paste layer** that sits on those pages — you keep chatting; it waits next to you so you can write.

## 📦 What to install

1. **Browser extension** (Chrome / Edge): floating button and sidepanel on AI sites, plus copy and Precise Chat. Install: [thinknote.pro/install](https://thinknote.pro/install)
2. **Word / WPS add-in**: for most people who already live in Word or WPS. Chat, then paste it back into the work you were already doing. Not something you only install on deadline day. A lot of the job just has to be fast. It doesn't need a deep think every time. Supports desktop Word (Windows / Mac) and WPS Writer on Windows; WPS needs one extra step: click **one-click paste**, then press Ctrl+V. Notes: [thinknote.pro/word](https://thinknote.pro/word)

Word on the web and WPS in the browser can't help you yet. The extension covers chat and notes. If you need it in desktop Word / WPS, install the matching add-in too.
