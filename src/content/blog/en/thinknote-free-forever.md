“Free forever” is easy to hear as “every feature, no bill, ever.” That is not the deal. Here is the boundary, then how invites fill the part that actually unlocks.

## What a free account includes

You do not need a subscription for local notes, a small cloud allowance (about **30MB** by default), and daily caps on:

- **Precise Chat**
- **Export**
- **Word / WPS one-click paste**

Those three default to **3** uses per day each. Operations can change the numbers; trust the usage card in your account.

The notebook is stored in the browser. **You can write offline.** Syncing across devices is what consumes cloud quota.

## Invite five people — what that unlocks

The default growth campaign threshold is **five qualifying invites**. After that, the three items above become **unlimited for life, with no subscription**.

Each successful invite also bumps your daily caps immediately (default +5 on Precise Chat, export, and paste). You do not wait until you hit five.

A qualifying invite usually needs all of:

1. They sign up with your invite code;
2. They use Precise Chat once;
3. They export once (a Word one-click paste on their side can also count).

Same-device invites do not count. The campaign can be switched off or retuned. **Lifetime grants already issued are not taken back** when the campaign ends.

The usage area in the extension sidepanel says “Invite 5 friends” for lifetime unlimited. Open it and copy your code.

## Sync can still cost money

Lifetime unlock **does not include** large cloud note storage.

The default paid plan is about **$4.99 / month for 2GB of cloud sync**. While subscribed, Precise Chat, export, and one-click paste are unlimited too. If you mostly write on one machine, invites can be enough. If you need a lot of notes on many devices, sync is the layer you buy.

Current prices: [thinknote.pro/price](https://thinknote.pro/price).

## How to choose

```
One machine, occasional Precise Chat / export / Word paste
    → free caps + five invites can cover the core loop

Many devices, lots of notes, need cloud sync
    → pay for capacity; unlimited of the three is included while subscribed
```

“Free for good” means this: **ask precisely, export, paste into Word** can be swapped for a lifetime grant via invites. The notebook itself still works offline. The cloud is a different bill.
