You hit the fourth Precise Chat of the day. The usage card lights up. Export is the same. Paste into Word is the same. The draft is not done. The cap is.

The first thought is usually: so I have to subscribe?

Not necessarily. Want more uses? Invite friends. Each one who qualifies immediately adds a bit to your daily caps on **Precise Chat, export, and one-click paste**. Five friends, and those three become a **lifetime unlimited quota**: no subscription, and no daily cap. Cloud sync is separate. Offline notes on one machine don't need a subscription.

## 🎁 What a free account includes

You can use this without a subscription: local notes, a small cloud allowance (about **30MB** by default), and a limited number of daily uses for:

- **Precise Chat**
- **Export**
- **Word / WPS one-click paste**

Those three default to **3** uses per day each. The numbers can change; trust the usage card in your account.

The notebook lives in the browser. **You can write offline.** Syncing to the cloud, and lining notes up across devices, is what uses cloud space.

## 👋 Invite friends: more uses first, then a lifetime unlimited quota

Each successful invite immediately adds a bit to your daily caps (default +5 on Precise Chat, export, and one-click paste). You don't wait until you hit five. The extra starts now.

After **five qualifying invites**, those three become a **lifetime unlimited quota**. Not extra days. Unlimited, for good. No subscription.

A qualifying invite usually needs both:

1. They sign up with your invite code;
2. They used Precise Chat once and exported once; or they used one-click paste on the Word side

Same-device invites don't count. The campaign can be turned off or the threshold changed. **A lifetime unlimited quota already granted is not taken back after the campaign ends.**

In the extension sidepanel, the usage area shows “Invite 5 friends · lifetime unlimited.” Open it and copy your invite code.

## ☁️ Cloud sync is separate

The lifetime unlimited quota covers the three items above, **not** a large cloud-sync allowance.

The default paid plan is about **$4.99 / month · 2GB of cloud sync**. While subscribed, Precise Chat, export, and one-click paste are unlimited too. If you mostly take notes offline on one computer, invites can be enough — no subscription. If you need a lot of notes across devices, sync is the layer you buy.

Current prices: [thinknote.pro/price](https://thinknote.pro/price).

## 🧭 How to choose

```
One machine, occasional Precise Chat / export / Word paste
    → free daily caps + invite friends. Five qualifying invites unlock a lifetime unlimited quota; the core loop can stay free

Many devices, lots of notes, need cloud sync
    → pay for cloud capacity; unlimited of the three comes with the subscription
```

“Free for good” means this: **ask precisely, export, paste into Word** — those three can be swapped, via invites, for a **lifetime unlimited quota**. The notebook itself still works offline. Cloud sync is separate.
