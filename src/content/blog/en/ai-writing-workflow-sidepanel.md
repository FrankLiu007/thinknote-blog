A lot of writing sessions look like one chat box: generate, dislike, generate again. The thread gets longer. Your judgment gets thinner.

A sturdier layout is boring: **AI page on the left, ThinkNote sidepanel on the right.** Chat stays chat. Arrangement stays arrangement.

This is not a built-in “send this question to every model at once.” The extension will not fan out for you. It only keeps the sidepanel alive while you switch among ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Doubao, DeepSeek, Yuanbao, Qianwen, and Yiyan — so **you** can carry the same question across.

![AI chat on the left, sidepanel on the right (screenshot pending)](/images/uploads/placeholder-sidepanel-split.png)

## A suggested loop

1. **Write the question in the sidepanel first.** Write the sentence you are actually stuck on. Do not demand a finished draft from the first prompt.
2. **Copy that same question into the first model.** Read it through. Mark lines that land or feel off.
3. **Switch models, paste the same sentence.** Listen for disagreement, not for polish. Who filled a blind spot, who invented a detail — that goes in the note.
4. **Chase only one to three subtopics you care about.** Drop the rest. Attention is scarcer than models.
5. **Write the conclusion in your own words.** Pasted paragraphs are raw material. The sentence you would sign is the draft.

This is the same Chat → Think → Note loop as [How to Upgrade Your Brain with AI](/en/blog/upgrade-your-brain-with-ai), with Note pinned to the right edge of the screen.

## Why compare models instead of staying in one thread

A single model is good at making a draft sound like a conclusion. A second model shows you:

- different structure (who defines first, who examples first);
- different facts (numbers, citations, steps that do not match);
- different tone (over-certain vs. hedged).

Your job is not to pick a winner. It is to write down the disagreements that matter for **this** piece. The sidepanel is the comparison table.

For formula-heavy follow-ups, use Precise Chat — [Learn STEM with AI](/en/blog/learn-stem-with-ai). When a spark shows up, [note as you chat](/en/blog/note-as-you-chat). When the draft has to become a document, [one-click paste](/en/blog/paste-ai-chat-into-word).

## A small checklist

```
Question lives in the sidepanel (yours)
    → same question to model A
    → same question to model B
    → disagreements into the note
    → one sentence you would sign
    → Word only if you need to hand it in
```

Writing software will not finish the thinking. It only stops chat from shoving writing off the screen. Keep arguing on the left. Keep the sentence you would put your name on, on the right.
