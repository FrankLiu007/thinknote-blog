Chat history is excellent at keeping messages and terrible at keeping **the moment something clicked**. Threads pile up; the good sentence sinks. We already wrote the essay: [Stop losing great ideas in a sea of chats](/en/blog/stop-losing-great-ideas-in-a-sea-of-chats). This post is only how to land that moment in ThinkNote.

## Open the sidepanel before you keep talking

On ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Doubao, DeepSeek, Yuanbao, Qianwen, and Yiyan, ThinkNote puts a floating entry on the right. Open the **sidepanel notebook**.

The panel stays with you as you switch AI tabs. That is the physical condition for “note as you chat”: **conversation on the left, notebook on the right, no context switch.**

## Copy it in — then do not stop at paste

When the model writes something that lands, copy as usual (Ctrl+C / Cmd+C). SmartCopy tidies in the background. Formulas, tables, and code blocks can enter the note instead of collapsing into plain text.

![Chat content and the note editor, 1:1](/images/uploads/1-1copy.png)

Paste is raw material. Then do the smaller, more important thing: **write one sentence in your own words.**

- How did I think about this before?
- What changed?
- If I keep only one sentence, what is it?

A highlighted AI paragraph is not yours yet. The sentence you can say out loud is. The older essay splits the habit into catch → rewrite → put it where you will see it again. The sidepanel is that place.

## Local notes work offline; sync is extra

Notes live in the browser first (IndexedDB). You can keep writing without signing in and without a network. After you are online and signed in, sync uses your cloud quota.

Free accounts get a small cloud allowance (about **30MB** by default). **Offline notes do not require a subscription.** Cloud capacity is a separate bill — see [How to use ThinkNote for free, for good](/en/blog/thinknote-free-forever).

When a note has to leave your machine, export Markdown / PDF / DOCX from the sidepanel, or paste into Word with the add-in. That path: [Paste AI chat into Word](/en/blog/paste-ai-chat-into-word).

## A minimum workflow

When a spark shows up, do not wait until the thread ends:

```
Mark the sentence
    → paste into the sidepanel (raw material)
    → write one sentence of your own (the actual note)
    → keep asking, or close the window
```

The window can close. The sentence already has somewhere to live.
